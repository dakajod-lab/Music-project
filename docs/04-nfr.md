# 04 — Non-functional Requirements

_Status: Collecting. To be discussed in phase 4._

Non-functional requirements describe **how well** the app works rather than **what** it does.
They are measurable where possible.

## Usability (USA)
| ID | Requirement | How it is verified |
|----|-------------|--------------------|
| NFR-USA-001 | When the user must choose between a destructive action (e.g. replacing a recording) and cancelling, the two choices are clearly distinguishable, and the destructive one is never the default. | Design review + usability check (manual) |
